Privacy & Support

Irving College Baptist Church · Effective October 19, 2026

This app lets you watch our livestream and sermon library without an account. It has no advertising, analytics, tracking cookies, or viewing-history storage.

When you load sermons or play video, your IP address and requested content are processed by our streaming service to deliver them. The church does not retain visitor or IP access logs for this app.

The app does not request your name, email address, location, contacts, microphone, camera, advertising identifier, or payment information. We do not sell or share app activity for advertising.

If you email us, we use the information you choose to provide to respond. Do not include sensitive information. Contact us about support correspondence or privacy questions at icbcnextsteps@gmail.com.

LG's operating system and app store have their own privacy practices. This policy covers the church's app and streaming service.
