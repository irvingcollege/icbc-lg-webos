# Privacy Policy — Irving College Baptist Church TV

Effective date: October 19, 2026

Irving College Baptist Church provides this LG webOS TV app to let you watch its livestream and sermon library.

## Information handled by the app

You can use the app without an account. The app does not ask for your name, email address, precise location, contacts, microphone, camera, advertising identifier or payment information. It includes no advertising, analytics or tracking tools, sets no tracking cookies, and does not store your viewing history or a user profile.

## Streaming requests

To load the sermon catalog and deliver video, the app connects over HTTPS to the church's streaming service at `live.irvingcollege.church`. Your IP address, the requested catalog or video URL, and ordinary connection information are necessarily processed by that service and its hosting infrastructure to respond to the request. The church does not retain visitor or IP access logs for this app. The app does not create a persistent record of these requests on your TV.

We do not sell app users' personal information or disclose viewing activity for advertising. We do not integrate advertising or audience measurement services. The locally bundled HLS.js playback library is used to play video when needed; it is not an analytics service.

## Contacting the church

If you email us, you choose what information to provide. We use that information to answer your question or provide support. Please do not send sensitive information. You can contact us to ask about access to or deletion of support correspondence. Visitor/IP access records and viewing-history profiles are not retained by this app.

## Children and platform services

This app offers church programming for a general audience and has no accounts, chat, uploads or features that ask children to provide personal information. LG's operating system, app store, and other independently operated services have their own privacy policies and practices; this policy describes the church's app and streaming service.

## Changes and contact

We will update this policy and its effective date if the app's data practices change. The policy is available inside the app through Privacy & Support and on this public page.
