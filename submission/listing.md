## Full description

A Place of Hope for Warren County.

Connect with Irving College Baptist Church in Warren County, Tennessee, from your LG Smart TV. Watch our livestream during church broadcasts and browse our library of recorded sermons.

Use your remote's arrow keys and OK button, or point and click with the LG Magic Remote. On-screen controls let you pause, resume and retry playback. Recorded sermons also offer 30-second rewind and fast-forward controls.

No account or subscription is required. An Internet connection is needed to load sermons and play video. Live programming is available during church broadcasts; when a broadcast is not running, choose a recorded sermon.

Visit www.irvingcollege.church or contact icbcnextsteps@gmail.com for support.
